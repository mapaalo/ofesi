OFESI TEST COPY - start here
============================

Thank you for testing Ofesi, an offline office team for small businesses.

What you need
  - A Mac or a Windows 10/11 computer with 8 GB of memory or more.
  - Internet for the first start only. Ofesi downloads its AI model once:
    about 2.7 GB on computers with less than 12 GB of memory, 5.7 GB on others.
    Use Wi-Fi, not a phone bundle. After that it works with no internet.
  - 8 GB of free disk space.

How to start
  1. Unzip the file and open the Ofesi folder. Keep the folder somewhere
     permanent, such as Documents.
  2. Mac: double-click START-Mac.command
     Windows: double-click START.bat
  3. The first start downloads the model. If it stops, start Ofesi again and
     it carries on from where it stopped.
  4. Your browser opens Ofesi by itself. README.txt explains the rest.

Your computer will warn you the first time
  Ofesi is a test copy and is not yet signed with Apple or Microsoft, so the
  computer asks you to confirm that you trust it. Only continue if you got this
  file directly from Mapaalo Musonda.
  - Windows: on the blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click "More info",
    then "Run anyway".
  - Mac: do this once, BEFORE double-clicking anything. If a box offers
    "Move to Trash", click Done instead; Move to Trash deletes Ofesi.
      1. Open Terminal (press Cmd+Space, type Terminal, press Return).
      2. Type this, with a space at the end, but do not press Return yet:
           xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine
      3. Drag the Ofesi folder from Finder into the Terminal window.
      4. Press Return. Nothing is printed; that is normal.
    Now double-click START-Mac.command. If a box still appears, open
    Apple menu, System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
    "Open Anyway".

What to tell Mapaalo afterwards
  1. Your computer: Mac or Windows, the model or year, and how much memory.
  2. Any warning or error. A photo of the screen is perfect.
  3. How long the download took, and how long from double-click to "Ready".
  4. Give the Front desk three real tasks from your business. Was the work
     usable? What was wrong or missing?
  5. How long did one answer take? Was that acceptable?
  6. What would you pay for this, once, in kwacha?
