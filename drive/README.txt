OFESI - your offline office team
================================

1. Plug in the drive and open it.
2. Mac: double-click START-Mac.command
   Windows: double-click START.bat
3. Wait about a minute. Your browser opens Ofesi by itself.
4. Fill in "My business details" once. Then type any task at the Front desk.
   It goes to the right team member. You can also pick a team member yourself.
5. Close the Ofesi window (Terminal on Mac, the black window on Windows) when you are done.

No internet needed. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves the computer.
Your chats and business details are saved in that computer's browser.
Use "Back up to a file" to save them onto the drive, and "Restore from a file"
to load them on another computer.

Hand over and walk away
  Hand over as many tasks as you like. The team does them one at a time while
  you get on with your day. Finished work shows as "1 new" next to the name.
  Tasks still waiting when you close Ofesi carry on the next time you open it.

Repeating tasks
  Set Repeat to "every day" or "every week" before you hand a task over, for
  example five Facebook posts every Monday. Ofesi does it by itself whenever it
  is open and the task is due. Stop one under "Repeating tasks".

Internet (optional)
  Tick "Check the latest news online for this task" when you have internet.
  Ofesi then reads the latest news headlines on the topic and tells you which
  source each fact came from. It uses very little data (well under 1 MB a task).
  Only a short search phrase goes online, never your chats or business details.
  With the box unticked, or with no connection, Ofesi works fully offline.
On a Mac, the first start copies the engine (about 30 MB) into the Mac's cache folder.

Every answer has Copy, Print / PDF and Save buttons.

Good to know
  - Mac: any Mac from 2020 or later (Apple Silicon is fastest). 16 GB memory is best.
  - Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 16 GB memory is best.
  - Computers with less than 12 GB use the small model (models\small.gguf) when
    the drive has one. It is faster and lighter, and a little less capable.
  - Ofesi can make mistakes. Check figures, and confirm tax rates and fees
    with ZRA, NAPSA or PACRA. Have a lawyer review important contracts.
